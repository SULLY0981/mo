## About this game

**The night is endless. Your light is not.**

One hundred nights ago, the sun did not rise. The Order of Lamplighters kept their shrines burning against the dark, until one by one their lights went out. Now only one lantern remains: yours.

Walk into the fog with nothing but a lantern and a single weapon. Your weapons fire on their own, so your job is to move, dodge and choose. Every level-up offers new weapons and relics. Combine them into a build that can survive twelve minutes of an ever-growing horde, then face the Hollow King and bring back the dawn.

**Your light is the heart of the game.** The lantern is a real-time light that casts shadows through the fog. Enemies appear from the dark with glowing eyes, and anything inside your light takes extra damage.

**A Halloween land to explore**
- Wander the Graveyard, the Pumpkin Fields, the Dead Woods and the Chapel Ruins
- Smash glowing jack-o'-lanterns: Treat, or Trick?
- Watch out for Gourdlings, hopping pumpkins with a very short fuse
- Survive the Blood Moon of the final hour

**Rekindle the shrines, recover the story**
- Light lantern shrines for powerful blessings
- Each shrine returns a memory of the fallen Order, and of Aldric, the Lamplighter who stole the sun
- Read every recovered memory in your Journal

**Build your light**
- 7 weapons that grow over 6 levels, and 13 relics
- Awaken a maxed weapon with its paired relic
- Dash through danger
- 7 Keepers to unlock, each with a different starting weapon
- 4 boss fights with telegraphed attacks
- The Sanctum: spend Embers on permanent upgrades between runs
- 5 Nights of rising difficulty for the brave, plus the Endless Night after your first dawn

**Made for every player**
- Full controller support with Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo button prompts
- Rebindable keyboard and controller, vibration and deadzone settings
- Reduce flashing, screen shake and damage number options
- Graphics settings for low-end PCs and Steam Deck
- English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Polski, Русский, Українська, Türkçe
